# Community conduct

This project welcomes contributors with different levels of experience and different tools. Help people understand a technical issue without attacking the person who reported it.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, scope, and reproducible results.
- Assume corrections can improve the catalogue; explain disagreements clearly.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time.
- Disclose relevant affiliations when recommending a project.
- Keep private information and credentials out of public discussions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive material, close a thread, decline contributions, or restrict participation. Where practical, they should explain the conduct concern and apply proportionate action. This does not require repeating harmful material publicly.

For confidential abuse reports, use GitHub's reporting and blocking mechanisms. A public issue may raise a non-sensitive moderation concern; do not repost private or harmful content to make the report. This repository does not advertise an unconfigured private contact channel.
