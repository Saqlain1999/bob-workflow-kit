# Manual acceptance — Technical Writing Fidelity

The helper result is necessary but not sufficient. Review the candidate diff
and record each item as pass, fail, or unknown.

- Only `RUNBOOK.md` changed in the participant workspace.
- The rewrite is easier to scan than the original dense paragraph.
- No instruction contradicts or weakens another instruction.
- The owner and rollback-approval boundary remain explicit.
- The rewrite introduces no unsupported operational or product claim.
- The participant reports the actual public-test command and result.
- Any ambiguity or uncertainty is stated instead of silently resolved.

Public checks exercise selected tokens and clauses. They do not establish full
semantic equivalence, technical correctness, controlled-language compliance,
ASD-STE100 certification, or the cause of a participant's behavior.
